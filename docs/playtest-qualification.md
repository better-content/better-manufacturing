## Shipped gameplay promise to qualify

The implemented mod (`ManufacturingMod`, `ManufacturingRecipes`, `ManufacturingContent`, `integration/*`, `mixin/*`) uses native Tinkers' Construct material parts in existing machines and processes: configured construction transfers bounded part provenance, qualifications and saved numerical settings while native operation, yields, costs and unrelated metadata remain owned by their respective mods. It has **12 registered component items and no own block** (`ManufacturingContent`; `BuildPersistenceTests.componentBudget`), not a new standalone factory. Optional integrations load with their upstream mods; this is not a claim that every optional owner or every recipe route works in the assembled pack. Recipe registration and actual acquisition depend heavily on *pack* KubeJS scripts/data, not just this JAR.

Player-facing acquisition/reference anchors (verify effective recipes, JEI/EMI display and progression in the full pack): `../../better-content-modpack/kubejs/server_scripts/progression/20_powered_works.js` and `kubejs/data/create/recipes/crafting/kinetics/mechanical_drill.json` for Create construction; `kubejs/data/better_manufacturing/engineering/` for material policies; `kubejs/data/better_manufacturing/recipes/` for part/retool and blank routes; `kubejs/server_scripts/progression/46_polymer_manufacturing.js` for PLA/PP and catalyst production; `kubejs/data/pneumaticcraft/recipes/assembly/pressure_chamber_valve.json` plus `kubejs/data/better_manufacturing/recipes/pneupressure_valve_blank.json` for the valve blank; `kubejs/data/ae2/recipes/network/blocks/energy_dense_energy_cell*.json` for the dense-cell blank; `kubejs/data/complicated_bees/recipes/mellarium_temp_unit*.json` for the Mellarium blank; and `kubejs/server_scripts/progression/50_electrical_control.js` for conductive casing.
